# Pictures still needed

Weather, Nature, and Coins are ready to go as soon as there are photographs for
them. Drop each picture into `image-sources/<Topic>/` using the filename below,
then the topic can be added to `src/lib/topics.js` and the four language files.

Every picture should be one clear thing in the middle of the frame, on a plain
background, in bright even light, at least 800px across, saved as .jpg or .png.
Filenames are all lowercase and become the word the app speaks.

## Weather — image-sources/Weather/

- [ ] `sun.jpg` — bright sun in a clear sky
- [ ] `clouds.jpg` — white puffy clouds with blue sky around them
- [ ] `rain.jpg` — rain falling, or raindrops running down a window
- [ ] `snow.jpg` — snow falling, or fresh snow on the ground
- [ ] `wind.jpg` — a tree bending or a flag blown straight out
- [ ] `fog.jpg` — a misty field or road
- [ ] `rainbow.jpg` — a full arc against the sky
- [ ] `lightning.jpg` — one bolt against a dark sky
- [ ] `ice.jpg` — icicles or a frozen puddle

## Nature — image-sources/Nature/

- [ ] `tree.jpg` — one whole tree with its shape visible
- [ ] `grass.jpg` — an even patch of green grass, up close
- [ ] `river.jpg` — water flowing between two banks
- [ ] `flower.jpg` — a single flower in one bright colour
- [ ] `leaf.jpg` — one leaf on a plain background
- [ ] `rock.jpg` — one rock on plain ground
- [ ] `mountain.jpg` — a single peak against the sky
- [ ] `moon.jpg` — a full moon in a dark sky
- [ ] `sand.jpg` — beach sand, close up
- [ ] `sun.jpg` — the same picture as the Weather set, copied in

## Coins — image-sources/Coins/

Photograph each coin straight down on plain white paper, filling the frame. The
app shows every picture at the same size, so a learner cannot use size to tell a
dime from a quarter — the face of the coin has to be sharp and well lit.

- [ ] `penny.jpg` — 1¢, heads side up
- [ ] `nickel.jpg` — 5¢, heads side up
- [ ] `dime.jpg` — 10¢, heads side up
- [ ] `quarter.jpg` — 25¢, heads side up
- [ ] `halfdollar.jpg` — 50¢, heads side up
- [ ] `dollarcoin.jpg` — $1 coin, heads side up

Optional, if the class is working on money more broadly:

- [ ] `dollarbill.jpg` — a $1 note, flat and straight on
- [ ] `fivedollars.jpg` — a $5 note, flat and straight on

These are US coins. Another currency works the same way — the filenames just
change to match the coins being taught.
